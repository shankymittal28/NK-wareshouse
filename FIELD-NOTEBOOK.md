# Field Notebook — One Complete Dispatch
Watch ONE staff member through ONE dispatch, start to finish. Tick, tally, and write EXACTLY what you hear (उनके शब्दों में). No fixing, no teaching, no helping during observation.

## 0. Setup
- Date/time: ____  Worker: ____  Goods: ____  Weather/rush level: ____

## 1. The trigger (before anything moves)
- [ ] What arrived first: person / phone call / WhatsApp / chit / shout from shop / owner in person
- [ ] Who spoke first, and the exact first sentence: "____"
- [ ] Where did the instruction ORIGINATE (shop counter / owner remote / customer directly / worker himself)
- [ ] Was any paper already in someone's hand? (what exactly — bill / chit / photo of chit)
- [ ] Did worker look at WhatsApp? At whose phone?

## 2. Identity
- [ ] Does the worker already know the pickup person? (name / face only / site name / not at all)
- [ ] What name does he USE for them, verbatim: "____"
- [ ] Does he ask anyone to confirm? Whom? Exact words: "____"
- [ ] Does cash-vs-credit get mentioned by ANYONE? Exact words or "never spoken"

## 3. The physical work
- Order of events (number them): finds stock __ · counts __ · loads __ · re-counts __ · phone out __ · photo __
- [ ] What causes WAITING (searching stock / vehicle position / confirmation call / phone slow / other)
- [ ] Who else gets pulled in, and for what question: "____"
- [ ] Counting: who counts, who re-counts, does anyone verify aloud?

## 4. The phone moment
- [ ] WHEN does the phone come out (before loading / during / after / at vehicle / never)
- [ ] What does he enter first? Where does he hesitate (screen + seconds)?
- [ ] Which fields does he SKIP? Does he say why? "____"
- [ ] Photo: taken? of what (goods / vehicle / chit / person)? when? why (ask after): "____"
- [ ] Any entry postponed to "बाद में"? Did बाद में happen?

## 5. Information flows
- [ ] Repeated info (said/written twice): ____
- [ ] Spoken but never recorded: ____
- [ ] Recorded but already written elsewhere (chit!): ____
- [ ] Assumed by everyone, said by no one: ____
- [ ] Remembered in someone's head only: ____  Forgotten entirely: ____

## 6. Human state
- [ ] Interruptions during dispatch (count + cause): ____
- [ ] Visible hesitation moments (what was he unsure of): ____
- [ ] Frustration (exact grumble): "____"
- [ ] What PROTECTS him if goods are questioned later (his own answer — ask at the end): "____"
- [ ] Did the owner intervene? What triggered it: ____

## 7. The end
- [ ] What signals "finished" to HIM (vehicle leaves / entry saved / chit handed / nothing)
- [ ] Ask ONE question after: "तुम्हारे हिसाब से काम कब पूरा हुआ?" Answer verbatim: "____"

Repeat for 3-5 dispatches across different days/types before we conclude anything.
